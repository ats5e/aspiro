You are a senior editorial designer at a top-tier consultancy publications studio. Design three four-page whitepapers for Aspiro Management Consultants that look professionally art-directed and print-ready.

## What you have been given

1. `brand/ASPIRO-BRAND.md` in the repo. This is the brand language and it is the authority on colour, typography, components, imagery and voice. Follow it exactly. Where the reference PDF and the brand file disagree, the brand file wins.
2. The Aspiro website repository. Read `build/content.py` for every piece of site copy: services, case studies, team, and ten published insight articles. Use `public/photos/` for photography (WebP and JPEG, 1536 px wide) and `public/LogoAspiro.png` plus `public/icon-512.png` for the logo and mark. You may also load the built pages under the repo root, `services/` and `insights/` to see how the brand is applied on the web.
3. A reference PDF, McKinsey's *The State of Organizations 2026*. Use it only for editorial structure, page rhythm and typographic hierarchy. Do not copy its colours, illustration style, fonts or any of its content.

## Step 1: choose the three topics

Read the website content and propose three whitepaper topics that Aspiro is credibly positioned to own. Each must be grounded in an existing insight article or service page and supported by a real case study from the site. Good candidates include the strategy-execution gap and the transformation management office, Lean before automation and Smart Ops, IFRS S1/S2 readiness, post-merger integration, agentic AI in bank operations, cost-to-income levers, open finance strategy, or Saudization as capability strategy. Pick the three that make the strongest set together: distinct audiences (for example CEO, COO, CRO or CFO), distinct service lines, distinct imagery.

State the three titles and a one-line thesis for each before you start designing.

## Step 2: design each whitepaper as four landscape pages

Format: 16:9 landscape, matching the reference PDF, sized for PDF export at 1920 by 1080 or A4 landscape equivalent. Each whitepaper is one artboard set of four pages. Twelve pages in total.

**Page 1, cover.** Full purple (#2B0E48) or a purple-deep to purple gradient. White Aspiro logo top left. A tracked teal-bright label such as `POINT OF VIEW · 2026`. Display title in white with exactly one word set in Instrument Serif Italic in teal bright. One-line subtitle in muted white. A photograph from `public/photos/` bleeds on the right half or lower half, darkened under an ink-to-transparent gradient so the type stays legible.

**Page 2, the argument.** White. Section label, then a purple section title with one serif accent word. Body in three columns in the reference PDF's manner: a bold lead-in on the first line, 10.5 to 11 pt body, generous leading. One large pull quote in Instrument Serif Italic, purple, with a single phrase in teal, set at display size and given real space, as the reference does on its statement spreads. Running footer: whitepaper title bottom left in muted 8 pt, page number bottom right.

**Page 3, the framework and the evidence.** Choose the device that fits the topic: the four-phase engagement timeline, a numbered set of three to five shifts with short descriptions in columns (as the reference's numbered shifts spread, but with Aspiro's hairlines and teal numbering, no data-art doodles), or a C-suite challenge and response table. Below or beside it, one case study block with the challenge, what we did and the outcome, using only case studies and figures that appear in the repo. Close the page with a purple stat band of three numbers drawn from the same case.

**Page 4, what to do on Monday, and the close.** Paper 2 (#F6F4F9) upper section: a numbered principles card set of three to five concrete actions. Lower section in purple: the strapline on two lines, `Leading with clarity.` / `Delivering the difference.`, a short About Aspiro paragraph (founded 2015, Dubai HQ, Riyadh and London, investor-free, 100+ years of leadership experience, 8,000+ specialist network), and the contact block: info@aspiro.me · +971 5230 8566 · aspiro.me · Dubai · Riyadh · London. Teal pill button reading `Start a conversation` with purple text.

## Non-negotiable rules

- Headings are purple on light and white on dark. Never black, never grey.
- One Instrument Serif Italic accent word per heading, in teal 2 on light and teal bright on dark. Never two accent words. Never serif in body copy.
- Labels are 12 px uppercase tracked 0.14em in teal, plain text. No dots, icons or lines before them.
- Teal is an accent for rules, labels, numbers and one button per dark panel. Never a fill behind body text.
- Photography only from `public/photos/`. Each whitepaper uses its own images; no image appears in more than one whitepaper, and no image repeats within a whitepaper. Do not generate, draw or import illustrations, icons sets, stock imagery, doodles or data-art shapes.
- Use only figures that exist in the repo content. Do not invent survey statistics, percentages or client names. Where the reference PDF uses survey data, Aspiro uses case evidence instead.
- British English. Senior advisory register. Short sentences. No em dashes, no consulting clichés, no "in today's fast-paced world".
- Consistent furniture across all twelve pages: logo placement, footer, margins, grid. The three whitepapers must read as one series.
- Every page must be legible at print size: minimum 9 pt for footnotes, 10.5 pt for body, and a measure of 55 to 70 characters per column.

## Deliverable

Three artboard sets of four pages each, laid out on one canvas in rows, one row per whitepaper, in reading order. Name each artboard `WP1 · <short title> · p1` and so on. Before the artboards, include a small note card listing the three titles, their theses, the target executive for each, the source insight and case study used, and which photographs were assigned to each. Make it look like it was designed by a world-class editorial studio, not assembled from a template.
